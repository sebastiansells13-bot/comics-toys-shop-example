## Deliberately not used

- **Real comic book cover photos.** Commons has almost no generic "stack of comics"
  photography (it's an encyclopedia media repository, not a stock site, and comics are
  heavily copyrighted so casual photos of them are rare). More importantly: even a
  freely-licensed *photograph* of a comic book cover doesn't grant reproduction rights
  to the copyrighted cover art and characters underneath it — photographing a
  copyrighted work doesn't launder its copyright. Real product photography for a real
  shop's own inventory is exactly right here; a demo reusing someone else's copyrighted
  comic art is not. Hence the original illustrated graphic instead.
- **A specific branded collectible** ("Robby the Robot," a well-known licensed movie
  character toy) that turned up in an early search — skipped for the same reason:
  a real trademarked/copyrighted character design, not generic vintage-toy imagery.

Each real photo used above was resized/cropped (no other edits) from the original;
originals are linked above.
